About Me

My name is Kokou Tozo, and I am from Togo, West Africa. I am currently studying Web Development at BYU-Idaho.

Web development has been a passion of mine for many years. I started creating static web pages in the early 2000s, and I have always enjoyed learning about new technologies and improving my technical skills. Studying at BYU-Idaho has provided me with a great opportunity to expand my knowledge and take the next step toward building dynamic and interactive web applications. Professionally, I work in the logistics industry, and I am particularly interested in participating in projects related to logistics, technology, and process improvement. I am always eager to learn, share knowledge, and collaborate with others. I enjoy working in teams and connecting with people who are passionate about technology and innovation. In my free time, I enjoy reading, walking, and spending time with my family. I am also an active member of my local church community, where I volunteer and participate in various activities.

I look forward to meeting you, learning from your experiences, and collaborating on future projects.
